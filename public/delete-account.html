Account & Data Deletion

Delete Your MindFort Account & Data

Developer: Fort Tech  |  App: MindFort (com.aistudio.mindfort.habits)  |  Last Updated: November 20, 2026

This page explains how you can permanently delete your MindFort account and all associated personal data managed by Fort Tech. You can delete your account immediately inside the MindFort Android app or submit a deletion request without needing the app installed.

Option 1: Delete Your Account Directly Inside MindFort (Instant)

  1. Open the MindFort app on your Android device and make sure you are signed in.
  2. Tap the Profile tab in the bottom navigation bar.
  3. Scroll to the bottom of the Profile screen and tap Delete Account.
  4. Review the confirmation prompt (and verify your Google Sign-In or account password if asked).
  5. Tap Delete Forever to immediately erase your Firebase Authentication account, cloud records, and local device cache.

Option 2: Request Account Deletion Online (No App Required)

If you have uninstalled MindFort or can no longer access your device, submit a deletion request using the form below or email shahaliakbar2007@gmail.com from the email address associated with your MindFort account.

Important Note About Active Subscriptions: Deleting your MindFort account removes your app profile and habit data, but it does not automatically cancel an active recurring Google Play subscription. To avoid future renewals, cancel your subscription in Google Play Store → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions before or after deleting your account.
